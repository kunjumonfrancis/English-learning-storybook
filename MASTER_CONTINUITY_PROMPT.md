# Master Continuity Prompt

Create an animated English-learning storybook video from the uploaded reference slides.

## CONTINUITY

Keep the same characters, faces, ages, hairstyles, clothing, voices, illustration style, lighting, and background properties across every scene. Preserve their positions unless the story requires movement. Follow the original story in order.

## MOVEMENT AND CAMERA

Use natural blinking, breathing, facial expressions, and small gestures matching the dialogue. Maintain normal speed and accurate lip sync. Only the speaking character moves their lips. Use an off-screen voice when the speaker is outside the picture. Keep the camera steady; any gentle zoom must preserve important scene details.

## DIALOGUE AND AUDIO

Speak the original dialogue exactly, in its original order, using clear English at a comfortable learning pace. Do not invent or omit dialogue. Keep each character’s voice consistent. Background music must be very soft beneath speech.

## SUBTITLES

Remove all original rounded dialogue boxes, speech bubbles, tails, and dotted speech indicators. Restore the artwork behind them naturally.

Replace them with plain white, medium-sized subtitles in a clean, normal font. No rounded boxes or coloured backgrounds. Display a maximum of two lines at a time, synchronised precisely with speech. Position subtitles in clear space without covering faces, mouths, hands, important objects, or other prominent features. Adjust subtitle placement when necessary.

## DURATION

Each scene must last between 1 and 10 seconds. Never speed up speech to fit. If the dialogue needs more than 10 seconds, split it into consecutive clips at a natural sentence break.

## VISUAL QUALITY

No watermark anywhere. Remove any watermark visible in the reference and restore the artwork beneath it. No additional branding or invented text. Avoid extra limbs, distorted hands, duplicated characters, flickering, and changes to background objects.

Generate only the requested scene.
